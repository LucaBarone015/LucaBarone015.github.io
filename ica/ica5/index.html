The Flexbox layout supports the user's experience by making the website more enjoyable to look at. It allows you to use design principles to help your site be more engaging and accessible. Having users click to reveal information gives them a choice to only see what they are interested in. They are no longer required to read everything, just what they want. I think collapsed content may disadvantage people using screen readers to navigate. They may miss content because it is initially hidden.

Hiding and revealing content allows the developer to better guide the user through the site. When choosing what to immediately reveal, you want to think about if it's necessary for every person using the site to read it, or is it's something only people who want to see more should access. This lets users have a more personalised experience so they feel welcomed and are more likely to return.
